# 👨🏻‍💻 Matteo Peixoto

## _`Data Analysis & AI | Electrical Engineer | Web Developer `_

My name is Matteo Peixoto, I’m 26 years old and based in Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil. I’m an Electrical Engineering student at UFRN and hold a technical diploma in Electrotechnics from IFRN. I’m constantly improving my skills in web development and data analysis, always eager to learn new technologies. My background includes hands-on experience with electrical projects, solar photovoltaic (PV) systems, and lightning protection systems (LPS), alongside technical analysis and process organization. Driven by a passion for technology, I am currently focusing on software and engineering project development to build my career across both fields.
